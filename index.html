Recovery Guide is a Pro feature
Get a step-by-step action plan to recover your lost or stolen device, including carrier block guides and police report tips.
Lost Device Recovery Guide
PRO
1
Act immediately — within the first hour
The first 60 minutes are critical. Use another device to remotely lock or wipe yours. iPhone: iCloud.com → Find My. Android: android.com/find. Samsung: findmymobile.samsung.com.
2
Change all passwords immediately
Change passwords for your email, banking apps, and social media. Enable two-factor authentication on all accounts if not already done.
3
Contact your mobile network operator
Call your carrier (Vodacom, MTN, Cell C, Telkom) and report the IMEI number. They can block the device from all networks, making it unusable even with a new SIM.
4
File a police report
Visit your nearest SAPS station. Bring your IMEI number, proof of purchase, and serial numbers. Get your case number — you need it for insurance claims.
📋 You can link your police case number to your stolen report inside OmniBackup.
5
Contact your insurer
Contact your insurer with the police case number, IMEI/serial number, receipt (from your OmniBackup vault), and policy number. Most claims must be submitted within 48 hours.
6
Check second-hand markets
Monitor OLX, Facebook Marketplace, and Gumtree for your device. If you find a listing, do not confront the seller — report it to SAPS with your case number.
7
Report to SAFPS
Register the stolen device at safps.org.za to prevent identity fraud. If your phone had banking apps or personal documents, this step is critical.
